Activate your license

  1. Copy your license key. It is shown on the receipt page and sent to you by email. Keep that email — the key is the only proof of purchase, and you will need it again if you reinstall Obsilide or clear its data.
  2. Open Obsilide and go to Settings → Premium → Activate license.
  3. Paste the key and tap Activate. This is the one moment the app goes online.
  4. That's it. Premium unlocks on that installation and keeps working offline — the app never checks again.

Activation is per installation. There is no device limit and the license does not expire.

What Premium unlocks

Eight notebook cover styles — Coral, Amber, Forest, Ocean, Indigo, Plum, Graphite, and Sand — and more local Premium features as they are added. Existing covers stay visible whether or not Premium is active; only changing one is gated.

The free notebook is unchanged and stays free. Hosted AI, sync, publishing, and storage are not included in this purchase.

No key in your inbox?
